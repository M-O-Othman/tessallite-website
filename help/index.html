Commercial path

Community first, Enterprise when rollout pressure appears.

Community is a self-hosted evaluation with scale limits covering the core platform capabilities. Enterprise removes operating limits and adds deployment options, support SLA, SSO, and security evidence. Some connectors and integrations are in beta or preview in both editions.
