Licensing

Community is free to run. Source-available is not the same as open-source.

Tessallite separates runtime use from source-code rights. Community Edition is the free self-hosted product you can evaluate and run within its signed licence limits. Source-available means selected source files can be viewed and studied, but they are not released under an open-source licence like Apache, MIT, GPL, or BSD.

Community Edition

Community Edition is the free, self-hosted edition activated by a signed local licence file. It is designed for evaluation, proof of value, training, development, and small internal use.

  • Full product surface for evaluation.
  • Control-plane limits such as tenants, users, and models.
  • No public claim of open-source rights.

Enterprise Edition

Enterprise is the commercial path for production teams that need unlimited scale, deployment support, SSO, support SLA, and security evidence.

  • Custom annual licence.
  • Production packaging and deployment options.
  • Commercial support and assurance material.

Source-available code

Some Community source may be published for review and internal evaluation. That does not mean the code can be freely redistributed, resold, hosted as a service, or used to create a competing product.

Where the legal files live

The detailed legal documents are under the website legal folder and are linked from the legal page. The version shipped with a signed release bundle remains the authoritative copy for that release.
