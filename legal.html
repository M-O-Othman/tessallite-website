Licence and legal

Clear terms for Community evaluation and Enterprise rollout.

Tessallite Community Edition is the free self-hosted edition activated by a signed local licence. Enterprise is the commercial route for production scale, support, SSO, deployment options, and assurance material.

The detailed legal documents are linked below and are also included with release bundles. These documents are product licence drafts for review; final customer terms should be reviewed by qualified counsel before public release or signature.

Community Edition

Free, self-hosted Tessallite for evaluation, proof of value, training, development, and small internal use.

  • Activated by a signed local licence file.
  • Full product surface with practical control-plane limits.
  • No query-volume, row-count, data-volume, credit, acceleration, or optimizer caps in the current draft terms.

Enterprise Edition

Commercial licence for production teams that need unlimited scale and formal rollout support.

  • Unlimited tenants, models, and users by agreement.
  • Deployment options, SSO, support SLA, and security evidence.
  • Commercial terms apply through the Enterprise licence and written order.

Source-available code

Source-available means selected source files may be viewed, studied, and used under narrow internal-use terms. It does not mean open-source. It is not Apache, MIT, GPL, AGPL, BSD, or OSI open-source code.
